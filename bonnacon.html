BONNACON

Of Asia is born a beast called bonnacon, with a bull's head and horns so curled inward they are useless for battle. When hunted, it flees — and while fleeing, its defence departs the other end, with such force and heat that it scorches whatever follows.

You are the bonnacon. The jet is your shield AND your gallop — but the gut runs dry. Eat ivy as you flee.
