## 📖 About

In medieval legend, the **Bonnacon** is a mythical beast of Asia with the head of a bull and inward-curling horns useless for battle. When hunted across the hills, it flees at breakneck speed — expelling a searing, caustic jet from its rear that propels it forward while scorching whatever follows!

Eat wild ivy, forage fiery golden thistles, and rocket-leap over obstacles in this fast-paced endless pursuit game.

---

## 🕹️ Controls (Mobile & Desktop)

- **Hold Screen / "VENTER" Button / Spacebar**: Loose the caustic jet to scorch pursuers and propel yourself forward.
- **Swipe Up / Tap High / "SALTUS" Button / W / Up Arrow**: **Leap** over boulders, humps, and pursuing hounds.
- **Aerial Rocket Boost**: Blast your jet while mid-air for a supercharged rocket leap!
- **Forage for Fuel**:
  - 🌿 **Hedera (Ivy)**: Restores +35% gut fuel.
  - 🔥 **Carduus Igneus (Golden Thistle)**: Activates 4 seconds of **Super Flame Jet** + unlimited gut fuel!
  - 🍇 **Bacca (Wild Berries)**: Instant speed burst and full gut recharge.

---

## 📱 Mobile-First Features
